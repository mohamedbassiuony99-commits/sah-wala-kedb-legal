Privacy policy

The short version

Player names, scores and settings stay on your phone. We never receive names. An account with your email is optional.

If you allow it, the game sends anonymous game stats and crash reports so we can fix problems and balance the photos. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

No ads, no tracking across other apps, no location, no microphone, no camera, no contacts. We do not sell data.

What stays on your phone

The names you type, the teams, scores, game history, your settings and the photos the game downloads. Deleting the game deletes them.

What we receive, and why

Only if game stats are on: a random device code made by the game (it is not your name, phone number or advertising ID), what happens in a game (for example a round finished, a category picked, truth or lie chosen, how long a round took), the app version and the system (Android, iOS or web).

Crash reports: the error message and where in the game it happened, so we can fix it.

We use this only to fix bugs, make the game better and decide which photos and rules work. The legal basis is your consent, which you give when you agree and can take back in Settings.

Where it is kept, and for how long

The data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers that may be outside Egypt, protected by access rules and encryption in transit.

Most game stats and all crash reports are deleted after 90 days. A few summary events (a game started, a game finished, a round revealed) are kept while the game runs, to see how it is played over time. "Delete my data" in Settings deletes this phone's device code, so nothing kept can be linked to it again.

If you make an account

An account is optional. If you add your email, we keep that email to sign you in and to keep your stats and purchases with you on a new phone. Sign-in codes are sent by our email provider (Resend). We never send you ads or newsletters.

Signing out keeps the account; "Delete my data" in Settings deletes it, including the email.

Pictures

Photos are downloaded from our server so the game has new pictures. Downloading them sends nothing about you beyond what any download needs (such as an internet address), which we do not store with your game stats.

Your rights

Under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) you can ask to know, see, correct or delete data about you, and withdraw your consent at any time.

To stop sending data, turn off game stats in Settings. To cut everything we received off from this phone, press "Delete my data" in Settings: it deletes the device code and what was stored with it. For anything else, contact us.

Children

The game is for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the game will ask you to agree again.
